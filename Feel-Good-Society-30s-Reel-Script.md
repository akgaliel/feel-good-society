# Feel Good Society — 30-Second Reel Script

**Format:** On-camera presenter · ~30 seconds · Social media (Instagram / TikTok / Facebook, vertical 9:16)
**Goal:** Touch viewers emotionally, show that joy and connection are good for your mental health, and drive ticket sign-ups.
**Tone:** Warm, tender, hopeful. Like a friend reminding you that you matter — and that you don't have to do this alone.

---

## The Script

### 0:00–0:05 — THE HOOK
**On camera:** Presenter close-up, soft lighting, gentle and sincere. Quiet, warm setting. Eye contact straight down the lens.

**Presenter (to camera):**
> "We all carry things nobody sees… and the hardest part is feeling alone in it."

**On-screen text:** `You're not alone. 💛`

---

### 0:05–0:11 — THE TURN (hope)
**On camera:** A small, warm smile begins. Behind the presenter, soft glimpses of people arriving, hugging, settling in.

**Presenter:**
> "But for one day a month, you don't have to be. You just get to show up — as you."

**On-screen text:** `Come exactly as you are.`

---

### 0:11–0:18 — THE JOY (benefit in motion)
**Visuals:** Tender, joyful B-roll — someone laughing till they tear up, a shared meal, live music, a hand on a shoulder, a guest speaker connecting with the room.

**Presenter (voiceover over B-roll):**
> "Good food, live music, games, guest speakers — and laughter that reminds you how good it feels to just be here."

**On-screen text (gentle fades):** `🎶 Music` · `🎤 Real stories` · `🍽️ Shared meals` · `😊 Real laughs`

---

### 0:18–0:24 — THE HEART (mental health)
**On camera:** Back to presenter, slower now, deeply sincere. Warm faces softly out of focus behind them.

**Presenter:**
> "Because connection isn't a luxury — it's how we heal. And caring for your mind? That's everything."

**On-screen text:** `Your mental health matters. Always. 💛`

---

### 0:24–0:27 — BELONGING
**On camera:** Presenter surrounded by smiling guests, a real candid moment of belonging.

**Presenter:**
> "Here, you're not different. You're family."

**On-screen text:** `A place to make real friends — no fear of being different.`

---

### 0:27–0:30 — THE CALL TO ACTION
**On camera:** Presenter beams, gestures "come join us." Logo + ticket info pop on.

**Presenter:**
> "Come feel good with us. Your seat is waiting."

**On-screen text:** `Monthly Dinner Event · $79` → `Tap to claim your ticket 🎟️`

## Quick Production Notes
- **Pace:** Open slow and intimate for the first two lines (let the emotion land), lift into brighter, quicker cuts at 0:11, then settle warmly again for the 0:18 mental-health line.
- **Music:** Start with a soft, tender piano/strings intro under the hook; bloom into an uplifting feel-good track from 0:11; pull back slightly under the "how we heal" line so the words breathe.
- **Captions:** Burn in subtitles for every spoken line — most people watch on mute, and these lines deserve to be read.
- **Faces over flash:** Lean on genuine, emotional close-ups of real attendees rather than busy wide shots — connection is the whole message.
- **First frame:** A sincere face + the line *"You're not alone"* — make it stop the scroll with heart, not hype.
- **Tone guardrail:** Keep it hopeful, never heavy. The feeling should be *"you're seen, and there's somewhere warm waiting for you,"* not sadness for its own sake.
